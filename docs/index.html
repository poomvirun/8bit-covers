8bit.science poster

8bit.science poster is a private tool used by the creator of the 8bit.science channel to upload their own finished 16-bit pixel-art explainer videos to their own TikTok account.

It logs in with TikTok Login Kit, shows the creator's account and posting options, and uploads one video at a time with the Content Posting API, only after the creator approves each post.
