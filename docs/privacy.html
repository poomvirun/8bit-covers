Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

8bit.science poster ("the app") is used only by the owner of the 8bit.science channel to post their own videos to their own TikTok account.

What we collect

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, username). No data from any other TikTok user is collected.

How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos the owner chose and to check their upload status. Tokens are stored privately and are never shared, sold or published.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the owner revokes access in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps) or asks for deletion via the contact below.
